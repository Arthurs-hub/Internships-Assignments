# ECONOMICS.md (Unit Economics)

## Lead Value to Credex
A converted lead (a startup that switches to Credex for AI credits) is worth an estimated **$2,500 - $10,000 ARR** in gross margin. 
- **Reasoning**: Average startup spends $2k-$5k/mo on AI infrastructure. Credex takes a 10-20% cut of the savings or a spread on the credits. At $3k/mo spend, that's $36k/yr. A 15% margin = $5,400 LTV.

## CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
Since the GTM is $0 paid budget initially:
- **Organic Social**: $0 (cost is founder time).
- **Referral Loop**: $0 (viral share URLs).
- **Partner Channel**: Potential revenue share with accelerators, but initially $0.
**Blended CAC Goal**: < $50 per qualified consultation.

## Conversion Rates for Profitability
- **Audit Completed -> Consultation Booked**: 5%
- **Consultation -> Credit Purchase**: 20%
- **Land -> Purchase (Full Funnel)**: ~0.15%
**Math**: 1,000 visitors -> 150 audits -> 7.5 consultations -> 1.5 sales. 
At $5k LTV per sale, 1,000 visitors generate $7,500 in value. As long as 1,000 visitors cost < $7,500 (which they do in organic), the tool is highly profitable.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M ARR at $5k average LTV:
- We need **200 active customers**.
- At a 0.15% full-funnel conversion, we need **133,000 unique visitors** total.
- That's ~7,400 visitors per month.
**What must be true**:
1. The viral loop (sharing results) must have a K-factor > 0.2.
2. The Credex credit supply must stay stable and discounted > 20% vs retail.
3. Pricing data stays current so trust remains high.
