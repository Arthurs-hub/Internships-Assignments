# ECONOMICS.md (Unit Economics)

## Understanding the Value of a Lead
For Credex, the goal of this tool isn't to charge users for audits, but to find startups that are spending a lot on "retail" AI services and convert them into Credex credit customers. 

A "converted lead" is a startup that decides to buy their Cursor, Claude, or ChatGPT credits through Credex at a discount instead of paying full price to the vendor. Based on average startup sizes (10-30 people), a typical customer might spend between **$2,000 and $5,000 per month** on AI infrastructure. If Credex earns a 10-15% margin (either as a spread on the credits or a service fee), a single customer generates roughly **$300 to $750 in gross margin per month**. This means a single loyal customer can be worth **$3,600 to $9,000 in Annual Gross Margin**.

## Cost of Acquisition (CAC) vs. Value
Since our Go-To-Market strategy relies on organic social media (X/Twitter), Reddit, and word-of-mouth (the "viral loop"), our initial **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is near $0** in terms of actual marketing spend. The only real cost is the time I spent building the tool and the tiny API costs for Gemini (which are currently covered by the free tier).

Even if we eventually spend money on ads, we can afford to pay a lot for a lead. If one customer is worth $5,000/year, we could technically spend $500 or even $1,000 to find that one customer and still be highly profitable in the first year.

## The Conversion Funnel (The Math)
To see if this tool is a "good deal" for Credex, let's look at a hypothetical batch of 1,000 visitors:
- **1,000 Visitors** land on the page.
- **150 Audits (15%)** are completed. These are "Warm Leads" because we now know exactly what they spend.
- **7.5 Consultations (5% of audits)** are booked for high-savings cases (those saving >$500/mo).
- **1.5 Sales (20% of consultations)** actually convert to buying Credex credits.

**Total Result**: 1,000 visitors → 1.5 customers → **$7,500+ in estimated annual margin.**
As long as it costs less than $7,500 to get 1,000 people to the site (which is easy with organic content), this tool is a massive "money maker" for the company.

## Path to $1M ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)
To reach $1,000,000 in ARR for this specific credit-reselling business line, the math is straightforward:
1. We need roughly **200 active customers** (assuming $5,000 average annual margin per customer).
2. To get 200 customers at our 0.15% total funnel conversion rate, we need to reach **~133,000 total unique visitors**.
3. Spread over 18 months, that is about **7,400 visitors per month**.

This is a very realistic goal for a tool that solves a real "pain point" (overspending) and has a built-in sharing mechanism. The beauty of this model is that the tool only needs to be built once, but it can continue to generate leads for months with minimal maintenance.

## Key Assumptions for Success
For these economics to work in the real world:
- **The Discount Must Be Real**: Credex must actually be able to provide credits that are significantly cheaper (>20%) than what the user currently pays.
- **Trust in the Logic**: If the audit math is wrong or looks "fake," users won't book the consultation, and the funnel breaks at the start. 
- **The Viral Factor**: If every 5th user shares their result, our "CAC" stays low because new users come in for free.
