# GTM.md (Go-To-Market Strategy)

## Target User
The exact target user is a **Founding Engineer or CTO at a Seed to Series A startup** (10-50 employees). At this stage, they are starting to feel the "death by a thousand subscriptions" but haven't hired a dedicated finance or procurement person yet. They care about burn rate but are too busy shipping to manually audit their SaaS stack.

## Search & Intent
Right before wanting this tool, they might Google:
- "Cursor vs Copilot for teams cost"
- "ChatGPT enterprise pricing for 15 users"
- "How to save on Anthropic API credits"
- "OpenAI enterprise vs team plan differences"

## Online Communities
- **X (Twitter)**: Founders following @levelsio, @shl, or @gregisenberg.
- **Subreddits**: r/startups, r/saas, r/indiehackers.
- **Slack/Discord**: YC Alumni Slack, Latent Space Discord, Lenny's Newsletter community.

## First 100 Users (0$ Budget)
1. **The "Benchmark" Post**: Create a "State of AI Spend 2026" thread on X using anonymized data from the first 5 test audits, tagging tool founders.
2. **Cold Outreach to "Just Raised" Startups**: DM founders on LinkedIn who just announced Seed rounds, offering a "free burn audit" as a value-add.
3. **Product Hunt Launch**: Launch mid-week with a focus on the "free tool" aspect.
4. **Tool-Specific Guides**: Write "How to optimize Cursor spend" on dev.to and link to the audit tool as the final step.

## Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex can partner with **Startup Accelerators (like YC or Techstars)** to offer the audit as part of their "Perks" dashboard. Since Credex already has the credit pool, the audit tool acts as the "Top of Funnel" that converts waste into Credex customers.

## Week-1 Traction Metrics
- 500 unique visitors.
- 15% conversion rate from Land -> Audit Completed.
- 5% conversion from Audit -> Credex Consultation booking.
- 20+ shares of public audit URLs on X/LinkedIn.
