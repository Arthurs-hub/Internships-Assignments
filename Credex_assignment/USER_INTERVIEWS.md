# USER_INTERVIEWS.md

## Interview 1: Mark T., CTO at Seed-stage Fintech
- **Quotes**:
  - "I have no idea what my team is actually using. I just see 'OpenAI' and 'Cursor' on the Brex statement."
  - "We have 12 people on ChatGPT Plus because I didn't know there was a Team plan."
  - "If I could save $400/mo, that's almost a whole new subscription for something else."
- **Surprise**: Mark didn't realize that ChatGPT Team actually has *better* data privacy than Plus.
- **Design Change**: Added a "Reason" field to each audit line to highlight not just savings, but feature/privacy gains.

## Interview 2: Sarah L., Founder at Nexus (Solo/Small Team)
- **Quotes**:
  - "I'm on Cursor Pro, Claude Pro, and ChatGPT Plus. I'm basically paying $60/mo for the same thing three times."
  - "I'd switch in a heartbeat if someone just told me which one is actually the best for Python right now."
  - "I don't care about $10. I care about $100."
- **Surprise**: Sarah is willing to pay more for a "best-in-class" tool but hates "redundancy" more than the actual cost.
- **Design Change**: Improved the Audit Engine to suggest "Consolidation" (e.g., sticking to one tool) for solo founders.

## Interview 3: David G., Engineering Manager (25+ people)
- **Quotes**:
  - "Procurement is a nightmare. I just let everyone put it on their individual cards and reimburse."
  - "Does this tool handle API spend? That's our biggest black hole."
  - "Credex credits sound interesting, but I need to know it's not a scam."
- **Surprise**: The fear of "scams" or "shady credits" is the biggest blocker for using Credex.
- **Design Change**: Added "Defensible Logic" and "Source Citations" to the results to build trust.
